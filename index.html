About Vijay Rehab Healthcare

Advanced Artificial Limb & Orthotic Rehabilitation Centre in Ranchi

At Vijay Rehab Healthcare, we believe every individual deserves the freedom to move naturally and live without limitation. Located conveniently at Devkamal Hospital Lane, Itki Road, Ranchi, our center brings cutting-edge prosthetic and orthotic solutions directly to patients across Jharkhand.

Under the expert care of Dr. Vijay Kumar (Consultant Prosthetist and Orthotist), we combine advanced biomechanical technology with customized precision fitting. From lightweight carbon-fiber prosthetics to pediatric braces and diabetic foot care, our goal is comprehensive restoration and empowering gait training.

Custom Digital Fitting

Meticulous measurements tailored to fit your body's unique ergonomics seamlessly.

Advanced Bionics

State-of-the-art myoelectric arm and leg prosthetics for dynamic natural motion.

Post Amputation Rehabilitation

Guided therapy from stump care, shrinker application to full gait re-education.

Esthetic Silicone Aesthetics

Realistic silicone finger and partial foot restoration matching natural skin tones.
